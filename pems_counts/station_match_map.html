What the colours mean

Lookup existing: station is in the 2022 station-to-hwycovid lookup, which is kept as is.

Good match: new station matched to a 2022 freeway link within 100 ft (same route, direction and GP/HOV type). Several stations can share one link.

Over 100 ft: new station with no such link within 100 ft. Left out.

No match: new station with no candidate link for its route, direction and type.

Ramp / connector: not matched in this pass (mainline and HOV only).
